About this list

This page tracks EDM / house / bass / techno shows and festivals at It’ll Do Club, SILO Dallas, The Studio at The Factory, The Bomb Factory, Green Elephant, Sounders, CTRL Room, Deep Ellum Art Co, Ductwork at SILO, Toyota Music Factory, Dos Equis Pavilion, Dickies Arena, Coba, and any other DFW venue or festival booking electronic acts. It’s refreshed weekly to keep a rolling ~5-month window of shows. “Buy Direct” links go straight to the venue or artist’s primary ticket seller; StubHub / Vivid Seats links are resale backups in case the primary sale is sold out.
